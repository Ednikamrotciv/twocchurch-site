APPLY THIS UPDATE (322) — LOCAL + LIVE
=====================================
Why a live service still read "ended": on 11 Oct the Bushiri broadcast was
running on YouTube, yet the office reported "off". The channel's /live page
carries no `isLiveNow` marker on a bot-walled read — it only names the video —
so the old check called it off without asking further. What changed (note 322):

- Before anything is called off, the check follows the Live tab's video to
  its own watch page and reads the broadcast's on-air chrome there (`Live`
  indicator + `watching now` / `Started streaming …`, measured on the real
  service — each marker alone, and `"isLive":true` always, still mean
  nothing). The watch page's id comes from the address asked when YouTube
  drops the canonical link, and its headline from the player overlay.
- Unreadable pages still answer `unknown`, never `off` — at the channel step
  and at the watch step — so a hiccup can never end a service that is on air.
- The office reports the followed answer at once (note 318's door), and
  visitors keep trusting a fresh same-target report; the follow also runs on
  the deployed fallback scrape itself.
- Unchanged and still locked: everything from 320 (doubled-"@" healing),
  318 (office-reported on-air answer — keep a signed-in office tab open
  during broadcasts; short cadence, urgent Watch check, moved-channel
  re-check, button re-asks, offline Publish stamps), maintenance holds until
  the office publishes (plain reload, /admin clear); with no new update the
  copy stays on the last published; only an explicit office action lifts
  maintenance; sleep/wake re-serves the last publish whole; every Publish
  still folds live's newer words in.
- The pre-ship verifier's section P now locks the follow (marker checks plus
  a live run of the shipped parser on synthetic live/trap/ended pages) — one
  FAIL blocks the ship.
