APPLY THIS UPDATE (298) — LOCAL + LIVE
=====================================
This zip contains ALL fixes in one place:
- MAIN PHOTOS SECTION REMOVED. The "Main photos" block is gone from the
  office drawer (Home banner, Home banner for phones, Home page background,
  Welcome section photo, Worship band, Choir, Church building views,
  Giving photo). Those pages now show their original photos. Ministries
  and Leadership photos are untouched and still editable.
- SLIDES TAKE PICTURES ONLY. The announcement-slide upload no longer takes
  videos: the picker accepts pictures, pasted links are photo links, and a
  pasted video link is named as video with no broken preview. Films already
  published keep playing on the site until the office replaces them with
  pictures — nothing published was deleted by this update. Delete a video
  slide and publish to take it off the site.
- SIGN-OUT KEEPS LAST-PUBLISHED (from 297, unchanged). A changed/retired
  password still signs tabs out at login, publish and undo — but the
  sign-out ends the session only. Only a new update or an explicit office
  delete clears last-published.
- THE MAINTENANCE NOTICE CANNOT GO MISSING ON UPDATES. Iron rule, unchanged:
  NOTHING blesses a copy except an explicit office action on its own
  server (Review and publish → Publish, the Undo click, the bless, or an
  offline flush) or the live feed. New code always waits on the notice
  until the office publishes — on every update, every copy.
- A pre-ship verifier lives at src/tools/verify-update.mjs and runs
  before every update zip (one FAIL blocks the ship) — now also locking
  the Main-photos removal and the pictures-only slide upload.
- Everything from 297 and before, unchanged: desktop zoom scales the box;
  last-published wins (this zip ships no words); publish replaces outright
  with a ~15-minute server undo; no disk needed on Render; slide rhythm,
  plain Restore/Publish buttons.

4. npm run dev → http://localhost:5173/  (admin: /admin)

   right back") — this update holds until blessed. That check is the point.
6. #/admin → Tools → Restore the backup file → Review and publish → Publish.
   Local lifts to 298 and the notice releases. Hard-reload desktop + phone
   width to verify.
7. Removal checks: Photos & covers shows Ministries and Leadership only
   (no Main photos); the Announcement-slides row offers "Upload a picture"
   with a photo-link box; pasting a video link says slides take pictures
