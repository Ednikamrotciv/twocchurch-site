APPLY THIS UPDATE (297) — LOCAL + LIVE
=====================================
This zip contains ALL fixes in one place:
- SIGN-OUT KEEPS LAST-PUBLISHED. A genuinely changed or retired password
  still signs tabs out as before — at login, at publish and at undo — but
  the sign-out now ends the session ONLY. Held words, queued offline work,
  the cached copy, version/author/log and the publish stamp are untouched;
  only a new update's first boot or an explicit office delete may clear
  last-published. The session drop itself (saved password gone, role/name
  cleared) never paints, caches or rebases anything.
- STAYING SIGNED IN, STAYING PUBLISHED (from 296, unchanged). Drops, blips
  and restarts never sign the office out; only an explicit password denial
  ends a session; a generation mismatch re-proves instead of burning;
  version/author/log ride with live content, so empty answers blank
  nothing; reconnect converges in one pass.
- FILMS PLAY ON THE TURNING BOX, SILENTLY, with 3–10s thumbnails, no
  black-blank faces, and visitor controls on the visitor flat face only.
- THE MAINTENANCE NOTICE CANNOT GO MISSING ON UPDATES. Iron rule, unchanged:
  NOTHING blesses a copy except an explicit office action on its own
  server (Review and publish → Publish, the Undo click, the bless, or an
  offline flush) or the live feed. New code always waits on the notice
  until the office publishes — on every update, every copy.
- A pre-ship verifier lives at src/tools/verify-update.mjs and runs
  before every update zip (one FAIL blocks the ship) — now also locking
  sign-out-keeps-words, publish-denied-signs-out and server-denied-signs-out.
- Everything from 296 and before, unchanged: desktop zoom scales the box;
  last-published wins (this zip ships no words); publish replaces outright
  with a ~15-minute server undo; no disk needed on Render; slide rhythm,
  plain Restore/Publish buttons.
